## Key finding: the Nudge Framework

The *Ready for Dev - Nudge Framework* page is a written framework plus five worked nudges. A nudge is a **contextual decision aid**, not a blocking task. It appears as an **inline card at the top of the order list**, below the filter pills, and pushes the orders down. It never overlays or delays accepting an order (Principle 4).

Principles: (1) contextual nudge over promotion, (2) it must solve a captain problem, (3) right message at the right moment, (4) don't interrupt order-taking, (5) one clear action per nudge, (6) frequency limits, cooldowns, priority and dismissal learning, (7) no repeat after an explicit rejection (suppress for days), (8) a measurable success metric.

Triggers → nudge: rejected 3–4 good orders → Go To Area (pink, 'Add Areas' button, or saved-area toggles); 3 short last-mile orders → Stay In Area (blue); low demand and Auto Boost off → Auto Boost (yellow toggle); afternoon with Food delivery off → Food Delivery (yellow toggle). Success → green snackbar above the nav ('Go To Area is switched on'), the nudge dismisses, and the matching filter pill turns active.

**No blocking-task pattern exists in this file.** The only 'Orders will be blocked! Wallet balance is low' surface is the Home 'Take action now' card, built from the remote *Action centre* component.

## Pattern candidates

### Inline contextual nudge card in order list
- **When:** System detects a captain problem (rejections, low demand, idle, mealtime) and wants to suggest ONE filter/service without blocking order-taking
- **Structure:** Card at top of OA list (below filter pills, above order cards), pushes orders down: tinted bg per feature (GTA pink, Stay-In blue, Auto Boost/Food yellow) + 1px darker border, radius ~12, close × top-right, centred illustration/Lottie (width fill, max 360x360), bold centred title, 1-3 line body (truncate after 5 lines), ONE action: outlined pill button ('Add Areas') OR 1-3 white rows (icon + label + toggle). On success: green snackbar above nav + nudge dismisses + related pill turns active
- **Components:** Toggle, Button (secondary outline pill), Snackbar, close icon, Lottie/illustration slot
- **Evidence:** [5760:7665](https://www.figma.com/design/ytkTDONL0GBKq3mpKhZnve/?node-id=5760-7665), [5760:9361](https://www.figma.com/design/ytkTDONL0GBKq3mpKhZnve/?node-id=5760-9361), [5760:11085](https://www.figma.com/design/ytkTDONL0GBKq3mpKhZnve/?node-id=5760-11085), [5760:12789](https://www.figma.com/design/ytkTDONL0GBKq3mpKhZnve/?node-id=5760-12789), [5760:14494](https://www.figma.com/design/ytkTDONL0GBKq3mpKhZnve/?node-id=5760-14494), [5821:44691](https://www.figma.com/design/ytkTDONL0GBKq3mpKhZnve/?node-id=5821-44691), [5821:51797](https://www.figma.com/design/ytkTDONL0GBKq3mpKhZnve/?node-id=5821-51797), [5760:16521](https://www.figma.com/design/ytkTDONL0GBKq3mpKhZnve/?node-id=5760-16521)
- **Variations:** CTA button vs toggle rows; neutral white card (kitchen); Kannada wraps 2-line titles
- **Conflicts:** Kitchen 'What are you looking for?' multi-action card violates one-action rule (rejected); Service Manager uses a different nudge style (centred text + yellow pill CTA in empty state, or nudge card inside Services dropdown); Snackbar copy reused wrongly (Auto Boost success says Go To Area)

- **Structure:** Horizontal scroll pill row under OA header; tapping pill morphs it into a tab with attached panel (tinted per filter) over dimmed OA: optional helper text, optional nudge card, list rows (label + toggle, optional Delete), footer text actions ('+ Add Area', '× Stop Go To'); tap outside/pill closes; auto-dismiss after 20s inactivity
- **Components:** Filter Pills, Toggle, text button, overlay scrim

## Compared with Runway specs

- **BottomNavigation**: the Ready-for-Dev frames use a local *Bottom Nav Final* and *Order Button Animation - FNL*, not the Runway `bottom navigation`. Behaviour matches the Runway Order Button states: Default is grey, Hide is black with a ↓ (OA open), Incoming is yellow with a count bubble and ring animation (F1–F8 timing matches the animation spec). Item sets differ: the file mostly uses **2 items (Home + Orders)**, the combinations sheet shows Home/Earnings/Orders and Home/Rewards/Earnings/Orders, while the Runway samples use Home/Credit/Earnings. One frame, M1 Home/Off-Duty, is 360×775.
- **HomeOfOrders**: the final OA in this file has a header ('N Orders' + a remote ON/OFF toggle with a speaker icon), a side-bar of order tabs and a filter pill row. That matches the Runway composition. Differences:
  - Pills are Go To / Area Orders or Stay In / Short Orders / Services / Customers, while Runway has Services / Go To / Stay In.
  - The final pill panel is a tinted attached panel with toggle rows and text actions. The hidden Runway *Content* panel uses #ffb9bf and a 'Know more' button.
  - The empty state adds Searching / Refresh / escalation, which the Runway component does not have.
  - Snackbars for async toggles sit over the list.

## Conflicts and inconsistencies
- Go To Area turn-off confirmation uses a red snackbar, and errors appear both as an inline red card and as a top snackbar.
- 'Auto Lite is switched on' copy is still used after the rename to Auto Boost. The Auto Boost success frame 5821:50028 shows 'Go To Area is switched on'.
- System-check escalation appears in three forms: modal card (Refresh page), inline yellow CTA (Service Manager) and nudge inside the Services dropdown.
- Pill row labels vary between frames (Area Orders vs Stay In).
- Localisation pages are broken:
  - Hindi, Malayalam and Tamil show 'Empty' instead of the '+20' fare (wrapping vertically) and on the Orders pill.
  - Telugu OrdersFilter shows a raw `%1$d` placeholder.
  - Malayalam, Tamil and Telugu text renders blank (font or empty string?).
  - The Telugu OrdersFilter copy is missing 4 newer frames: the Customers filter (6265:5630, 6271:8284), the E-rickshaw card (6674:6524) and 6658:8299.
- Rejected or superseded designs: the Basic Flow v0 (blue Orders FAB in a notched nav, a Preference page, chip filters with ×, 'Order Settings'), the Kitchen 'rejected' section, the Kitchen note 'OA as a page / bottom sheet / floater', the ORB/PIP floating order bubble, and the Kitchen note 'Rejected for now because pull works better than push so no auto nudge for service'.
