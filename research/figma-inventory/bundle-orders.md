## How bundled (multi-order) flows are laid out

- **Order alert**: one bundle card on the home OA surface. It has a bundle header (`Bundle Order` chip, total `₹130+`, `(40 Mins)` or `(₹30/km)`, and a vehicle illustration with a `+2` badge) and two stacked order cards joined by a `+` connector. Order 1 is complete (pickup and drop). Order 2 is approximate (`₹60+` or `₹60-₹91`, area or cluster only). The footer has a reject ring with a timer, plus Accept. A side bar lists the other pending orders.
- **In-trip (M1)**: the captain completes order 1 as normal (pickup → wait timer → OTP → drop slider → payment). A positive snackbar on navigation says `2nd Order Assigned` (later marked for removal). End of Order swaps Done for `Go To 2nd Order` / `Go to next drop`.
- **Between orders**: home is dimmed with an overlay card that shows bundle progress. Order 1 shows ✓ Completed. Order 2 shows `Looking for nearby order` with an animated loader, a guarantee line, and `Don't want to wait? Reject`, which has loading and error states. The search then resolves to one of three outcomes: `2nd Order Assigned` with an OA Card and `Go to pickup`; a not-found modal with the promised-amount payout and `Okay`; or (Nov) a timed choice list of 2–3 exclusive orders with Exit bundle.
- **Multiple pickups/drops**: these are always sequential, never shown on one map. The pickup screen gains a queued-order card. Chained bundles (`2nd Bundle Assigned`, Order 2 + 3) reuse the bundle card.
- **Payout vs promise**: the Payment page shows `Paid to your Wallet +₹17` as a top-up. A cash-confirm bottom sheet follows, then End of Order with the earnings breakup.

## Conflicts / inconsistencies

- The bundle OA is a bespoke card. It uses the older `Service Chip` (not Runway `.Service Chips`) and a `Reject Button` from another library, while single-order OAs use Runway `OA Cards`.
- End of Order: Section 1 uses an old `CTA` component with value props. Section 6 uses Runway `Buttons` Large with an earnings breakup.
- Frame heights are mixed: 720 and 800.
- Copy is inconsistent: `2nd Order Assigned` / `2nd Order assigned` / `2nd Order is assigned` / `2nd Order is accepted`. The guarantee line also varies.
- Several components come from non-Runway libraries: `Toggle`, `stamp`, `Bottom Nav Final [3 - Orders]`, `Filter Pills`, `GTA Icon`, `Slider Buttons`, `CTA`.
- Navigation screens are static screenshots, not components.
- Two loaders coexist: a skeleton modal (515:278773) and a bundle-progress card (515:266357).

## UT / research notes (setup & Design UT)

- 7 auto captains tested 4 OA designs. The designer's first two read as bundles. Some captains thought there would be 3 simultaneous pickups, and some found the Order 1 card incomplete because it showed pickup only.
- The time hint `(50 mins)` helped captains link the total fare to time. Captains hesitated over the unknown next drops.
- Of the PM designs, #1 read as 2 separate orders and #2 was too detailed.
- Persona doc: non-daily captains think in time blocks. Positioning: 'Here's your next 60 minutes planned'.

## Localisation (tier C)

- Content request <lang> pages have 8 frames against 22 in the source. Only the early End of Order and Navigation frames are localised; the 14 `648:*` home-overlay and OA frames are missing.
- Hindi geometry matches the source, with no horizontal text overflow. The rating row sits below y=720 in both the source and Hindi (scroll content).
- Ready for Dev <lang> pages have 12 top-level nodes against 15 in the source. They were not read (timeouts, then quota). The pages likely predate the 3 Nov frames and Section 7 (exclusive orders); this is unverified.
