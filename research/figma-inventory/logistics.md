## Pattern candidates

- Structure: InternalPageHeader(back, title, Help) > hero image 360x202 > title Bold 24 > stack of TaskRow (gap16) > sticky footer: Primary Large 'Complete' disabled until all rows verified + optional helper text + Tertiary-Info small link with trailing arrow ('Apply for merch')
